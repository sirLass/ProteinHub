# Protein Hub - PRD: Roadmap & Scope

## 10. Phased Rollout Plan

### Phase 1 --- MVP (Months 1–3)
- Public landing page with teaser video and FAQ.
- Business presentation registration form.
- Basic admin dashboard: view registrants, send confirmations.
- Member portal with Module 1 and progress tracking.
- Subscription access control (Paid/Locked).
- Basic AI chatbot for guest inquiries.

### Phase 2 --- Growth Features (Months 4–6)
- Full multi-module training system.
- Recipe and resource library with search.
- Costing adjustment calculator.
- Referral link system with tracking.
- Admin reporting dashboard (conversions, churn, stats).
- Chatbot escalation queue with owner notification.

### Phase 3 --- Scale & Optimize (Months 7–12)
- Advanced chatbot: trained on full module content.
- Distributor performance leaderboard (optional).
- Mobile app wrapper (PWA).
- Automated renewal reminders.
- Regional content variations.
- Video analytics and heatmaps.

## 11. Out of Scope (Version 1.0)
- E-commerce / direct product ordering.
- Automated commission payouts (MLM-style).
- Native mobile applications (App Store/Play Store).
- Real-time video conferencing integration.
- Public-facing content on YouTube.
- Integration with external accounting/inventory software.
